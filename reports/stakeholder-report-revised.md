# Stakeholder summary — sales performance

## What the analysis shows

The reviewed dataset records 2.26M in sales value across 4,922 distinct orders from
2015–2018. Consumer contributes approximately half of sales, while West and East
together contribute approximately 61%. Technology leads the three product
categories, and Q4 contributes approximately 38.5% of recorded sales.

Annual sales declined in 2016 before increasing in 2017 and 2018. This pattern makes
the later growth worth investigating, but it should not be described as continuous
year-over-year growth.

## Business questions suggested by the findings

- Which products, customers, or seasonal events explain the Q4 concentration?
- Do West and East remain leading regions after accounting for customer count and
  order frequency?
- Which Technology sub-categories drive its category leadership?
- Are differences in shipping-mode usage driven by geography, segment, or order mix?
- Do high-sales customer groups also deliver acceptable margin and retention once
  those fields become available?

## Responsible recommendations

The results support prioritising these questions for deeper investigation. They do
not yet justify increasing advertising spend, changing inventory, or targeting a
segment solely on the basis of sales concentration. Those decisions require margin,
stock, campaign, and customer-behaviour data plus controlled evaluation.

## Limitations

This is descriptive portfolio analysis. It does not measure profit, ROI, conversion,
incremental lift, or implementation outcomes. Recommendations are hypotheses for
future testing rather than proven business actions.
