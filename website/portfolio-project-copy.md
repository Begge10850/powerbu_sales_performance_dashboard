# Sales Performance Analytics

## Short card copy

End-to-end retail sales analysis using Databricks, PySpark, Spark SQL, and Power BI.
The project validates 9,800 sales line items, defines order-level KPIs, and presents
regional, segment, product, shipping, and time patterns through a two-page dashboard.

## Case-study introduction

I built this project to practise the full path from raw operational data to a
decision-facing business-intelligence report. I used Databricks and PySpark to
inspect and validate the source, Spark SQL to answer ten business questions, and
Power BI to create an executive summary and interactive market breakdown.

## Key results

- 2.26M in recorded sales value across 4,922 orders.
- Consumer contributed approximately 50.8% of sales.
- West and East jointly contributed approximately 61.0%.
- Technology led categories at approximately 827.5K.
- Q4 represented approximately 38.5% of sales.

## What I improved after validation

I separated line-item and order-level metrics, corrected average order value,
introduced repeatable data-quality checks, and replaced a truncated visual-table
download with a validated programmatic export. I also documented the limits of the
data so that sales patterns are not incorrectly presented as profit, ROI, or causal
impact.

## Tools

## Honest scope

This is an independent descriptive analytics project, not a commercial live system.
The dataset does not include profit, cost, marketing-spend, or conversion fields.
